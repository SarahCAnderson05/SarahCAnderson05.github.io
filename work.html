wearable sensor fixtures

UCSD Wang Lab · CAD · 3D printing · prototyping

i work perform wetlab work, pharmacokinetic/pharmacodynamic modeling, and data analysis as an undergraduate researcher at Wang Lab (UCSD). i'm currently working on my fine motor skills (lots of pipetting and small sensors testing), developing strong understandings of electrochemical techniques including cyclic voltametry and research on wearable sensors. levodopa PK/PD modeling

MATLAB · compartment modeling · differential equations · electrochemistry

i developed a multi-compartment model to study how orally administered L-dopa moves through blood, tissue, sweat, and motor response pathways. i applied partial fractions, la'place transforms, and multi-compartmental ode functions to real-world systems. developed strong matlab and origin skills.
