me

Outside of school, I run, play violin and piano, take photos of landscapes and textures, and usually have some project, application, or half-finished idea going.

work

Wearable sensor fixtures

UCSD Wang Lab · CAD · 3D printing · prototyping

I work on CAD and 3D-printed fixtures and housings for wearable sensor research. This has introduced me to research prototyping, design iteration, and the process of turning lab ideas into physical devices.

CAD 3D printing research

MAE 3 robot project

mechanical design · testing · competition

I helped design, build, and test a competition robot for UCSD’s introductory mechanical design course. The project taught me how much iteration matters when ideas meet real hardware.

robotics mechanical design teamwork

Levodopa PK/PD modeling

MATLAB · modeling · data visualization

I developed a compartment-based model to study how orally administered L-dopa moves through blood, tissue, sweat, and motor response pathways. The project helped me connect engineering modeling with biomedical research.

MATLAB modeling biosensors

Satellite TLE analysis

orbital data · MATLAB · aerospace

I used satellite TLE data to compare orbital parameters like inclination, period, altitude, eccentricity, and mission type.

aerospace MATLAB data
